\begin{abstract}
Everything in this paper runs on a single consumer RTX~4080, and we use that
constraint as a feature: small-scale mechanistic science on a training pathology.
FiLM-style regime conditioning of a Mamba selective state-space model---a
per-block, channel-wise affine on each block's input, meant to steer the
input-dependent scan parameters $\Delta,\mathbf{B},\mathbf{C}$---is a
\textbf{decisive null}. Under joint training the modulation collapses to identity
and \emph{cannot be forced active} even by direct supervision. With an exact
zero-initialized control (modulated and unmodulated backbones are bit-identical at
step~0) and a forced-active, router-supervised escalation, the modulation decays
monotonically to identity on every objective and dataset we test. We explain it:
an input-side affine is a \emph{gauge} direction that folds exactly into the
block's own $W_\Delta/W_B/W_C$ projections, so joint training slides it back to
identity, with a dataset- and architecture-invariant signature
($\tau\approx7000$, $R^2>0.999$ across FI-2010, Kaggle crypto, and Polymarket)
that implicates the optimizer, not the data. An output-side placement makes the
null \emph{overdetermined}; a toy reference-scan demonstrates the in-kernel
boundary; a weight-decay ablation shows the decay is weight-decay-driven over a
flat loss; and a char-Mamba language-model control bounds the claim to
\emph{weakly-decodable} regimes. The financial datasets are a substrate, not the
subject: no backbone dominates a matched ablation, and the one surviving economic
edge is architecture-independent (a logistic reproduces it). The apparatus of exact
controls, forced-active escalations, and toy probes---affordable because each run is
small---is the contribution.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

MOSS celebrates rigorous science done at small scale, and this paper is in that
spirit: every experiment---training, escalations, ablation, and a synthetic
probe---runs on a \textbf{single consumer RTX~4080}, and the $\le 1$-GPU
constraint is what makes the study clean rather than what limits it. Selective
state-space models (Mamba)~\citep{gu2024mamba} have reshaped long-context
sequence modeling, but \emph{which forms of conditioning survive their training
dynamics} is poorly understood. We pick one concrete, widely-attempted
form---FiLM~\citep{perez2018film} regime conditioning of a Mamba backbone---and
diagnose, at small scale, exactly why it fails, then turn the failure into a
reusable methods package.

\paragraph{The object of study.}
We attach a \emph{RegimeFiLM} modulator (a router inferring a soft regime latent, a
hypernetwork emitting per-block channel-wise $(\gamma,\beta)$) to a Mamba
world-model backbone. Because the selective-scan parameters
$\Delta_t,\mathbf{B}_t,\mathbf{C}_t$ are input-dependent linear projections of each
block's input (\S\ref{sec:setup}), applying the affine to the block \emph{input} is
the natural, kernel-friendly way to push a regime signal \emph{into} the dynamics
without touching the fused CUDA kernel. The modulator is exactly zero-initialized,
so at step~0 the modulated backbone is \emph{bit-identical} to the unmodulated
one---a clean control.

\paragraph{The null, and the apparatus that establishes it.}
Despite that exact control and the strongest activation pressure we can
apply---forced-active initialization \emph{plus} direct router supervision on the
most defensible regime axis---the modulation decays to identity on every objective
and dataset (\S\ref{sec:null}). The mechanism makes the null worth a paper: an
input-side affine is a \emph{gauge} direction folding losslessly into the block's
own projections, so the optimizer slides it back to identity with a dataset- and
architecture-invariant signature ($\tau\approx7000$, $R^2>0.999$); the
\emph{output-side} placement the gauge argument names does not escape it, so the
null is \emph{overdetermined}. The small-scale apparatus is the contribution---an
exact zero-init paired control, a forced-active router-supervised escalation, a
weight-decay ablation, a toy reference-scan isolating the in-kernel boundary, and a
char-Mamba LM control that bounds the claim---all affordable because each run is
small.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1) A \textbf{decisive, forced-active null} for input-affine FiLM conditioning of a
Mamba selective scan, with an exact zero-init control, on three datasets and a money
objective (\S\ref{sec:null}). (2) The \textbf{gauge-absorption mechanism} and its
\textbf{dataset-invariant} signature ($\tau\approx7000$, $R^2>0.999$), plus an
\textbf{output-side placement showing the null is overdetermined}
(App.~\ref{app:mech}). (3) A \textbf{small-scale methods package}: exact-control
$+$ escalation protocol, a toy reference-scan demonstrating the in-kernel boundary,
a weight-decay ablation isolating the driver, and a char-Mamba LM control bounding
the null to weakly-decodable regimes (\S\ref{sec:methods}, App.~\ref{app:methods}).

\section{Setup}\label{sec:setup}

A selective SSM maps $x_t$ to $y_t$ via a latent
$h_t=\bar{\mathbf{A}}h_{t-1}+\bar{\mathbf{B}}x_t$, $y_t=\mathbf{C}h_t$; Mamba makes
the scan parameters input-dependent,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:selection}
\Delta_t=\softmax(W_\Delta x_t),\quad \mathbf{B}_t=W_B x_t,\quad \mathbf{C}_t=W_C x_t,
\end{equation}
so \emph{any} affine transformation of $x_t$ propagates into all three scan
parameters. The backbone is an $L{=}4$ stack of Mamba-3
SISO~\citep{lahoti2026mamba3} blocks; a router emits
$\bm{\alpha}_t=\softmax(W_r x_t^{(0)})\in\R^R$ and a zero-initialized hypernetwork
emits bounded $\gamma^{(\ell)}=1+\tanh(\tilde\gamma)$,
$\beta^{(\ell)}=\tanh(\tilde\beta)$, so $\gamma{=}\mathbf{1},\beta{=}\mathbf{0}$ at
step~0. The \textbf{input-side} site gates the block input
$\gamma^{(\ell)}\odot\RMSNorm(x_t^{(\ell-1)})+\beta^{(\ell)}$ (routing the regime
into $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ via Eq.~\eqref{eq:selection}); a
\textbf{post-scan} control gates the block output, sharing the identical modulator;
a Switch-Transformer load-balance term~\citep{fedus2022switch} (disabled in the
escalations) regularizes the marginal router entropy. Two diagnostics gate every
claim: $\mathrm{film}_g=\mathrm{mean}\,|\gamma-1|$ (zero iff identity) and the
router entropy $\mathrm{reg}_H$ (uniform maximum $\log R$)---load-bearing because a
gap measured with an \emph{inert} FiLM is a distributional artifact, not a
conditioning effect.

\section{The Null, and Why It Holds}\label{sec:null}

\paragraph{Joint training drives FiLM inert.}
Across five objectives on two datasets and a money objective, FiLM collapses to
identity: $\mathrm{film}_g$ decays toward zero and $\mathrm{reg}_H$ sits at its
uniform maximum (Table~\ref{tab:film_null}, App.~\ref{app:tables}). Where a
generalization gap reads positive (direction $+0.008$, settlement $+0.18$, by
sign), FiLM is provably inert, the treatment is \emph{absolutely worse} in both
regimes, and the gap is a high$-$low distributional artifact whose sign even flips
with head calibration---which is why the activation diagnostics are load-bearing.

\paragraph{Forced-active escalation (single dataset).}
The decisive small-scale move is to fight the optimizer as hard as we can and watch
it win anyway. We force FiLM active ($\mathrm{film}_g=0.150$ at step~0) and
supervise the router \emph{directly} on the spot efficiency-ratio axis with the
strongest settings (LR multiplier~50, supervision weight~30, decoupled
router/FiLM, fed observation volatility). The modulation still decays monotonically
($\mathrm{film}_g: 0.150\!\to\!0.119\!\to\!0.100\!\to\!0.074\!\to\!0.056\!\to\!0.043$,
still falling), $\mathrm{reg}_H=\log 8$ throughout, while a \emph{frozen} router
probes to $0.92$ agreement with the regime bucket---the capacity is there; joint
training will not use it.

\paragraph{Gauge absorption: why identity is a free direction.}
The input-side modulation $\tilde{x}=\gamma\odot\hat{x}+\beta$ is applied to a block
input the next operations re-project (Eq.~\eqref{eq:selection}). A constant
channel-wise scale folds \emph{exactly} into those projections as a
right-multiplied diagonal, $W_\bullet(\gamma\odot\hat x)=(W_\bullet\diag(\gamma))\hat x$,
and the shift folds into their biases, with \emph{no change} to the function the
block computes. Identity is therefore a \emph{gauge} direction---a flat, lossless
valley the optimizer slides along---which predicts a decay rate that is a property
of the parameterization, not the data.

\paragraph{The strongest-null escalation: a dataset-invariant $\tau$.}
We confirm that prediction with a \emph{cross-dataset strongest-null escalation}
(distinct from the single-dataset run above: a stronger $\mathrm{film}_g=0.250$
initialization, run on two datasets with the load-balance term disabled). The
modulation decays monotonically on \emph{both} ($0.250\!\to\!0.144$ Kaggle,
$0.250\!\to\!0.161$ FI-2010), $\mathrm{reg}_H\approx\log 4$ throughout. An
exponential fit $a\,e^{-t/\tau}+c$ matches both at $R^2>0.999$ with near-identical
parameters ($a\approx0.30$, $\tau\approx7000$ steps) and asymptote $c\le0$; since
$\mathrm{film}_g\ge0$, the modulation converges to identity within our training
horizon rather than to a positive plateau. That the rate is essentially
\emph{identical} across two very different markets points to the optimizer, not the
data (Figure~\ref{fig:escalation_decay}).

\paragraph{The null is overdetermined.}
The gauge argument names an obvious next test: gating \emph{after} the scan. Under
the identical escalation, the output-side gate does \emph{not} escape---each block
ends in a \emph{bias-free} output projection, so the output scale folds into it
($0.250\!\to\!0.147$ FI-2010, $0.250\!\to\!0.143$ Kaggle, same $\tau$, $R^2>0.999$),
while the one non-absorbable component (the additive shift) decays in lockstep
under reconstruction dominance. The null is thus \emph{overdetermined}: gauge
absorption removes every direction the host weights can represent, reconstruction
dominance removes the one they cannot, and a surviving modulation would have to act
\emph{inside} the fused kernel---outside our hardware envelope, so we delineate the
boundary as a location rather than a parameterization (derivations and the
output-side fit in App.~\ref{app:mech}).

\section{Small-Scale Methods That Establish the Boundary}\label{sec:methods}

The $\le1$-GPU budget is what lets us \emph{lose, repeatedly}, until the mechanism
is pinned down. Three elementary probes (full numbers in App.~\ref{app:methods})
turn the asserted boundary into a demonstrated one. \textbf{(i) Folding (toy
reference scan).} An input-affine modulation folds into primed host projections to
floating-point precision (max deviation $1.2\times10^{-6}$) and decays to identity
($\mathrm{film}_g: 0.086\!\to\!0.010$), while the \emph{same} modulation applied to
the projected $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ \emph{inside} the scan is
non-absorbable (residual $1.3\times10^{-2}$, $\sim10^4\times$ larger) and
\textbf{persists} ($0.093\!\to\!0.175$, seeds 0--3)---a modulation that lives inside
the kernel and survives the very training that decays the affine form.
\textbf{(ii) Gauge vs.\ regularization.} With weight decay \emph{off} the
input-affine is no longer pulled to identity (toy $0.086\!\to\!0.342$; full-model
$\tau$ from $\approx6685$, $R^2{=}0.9999$, to $\approx3\times10^5$, $R^2{=}0.48$),
so the decay is \textbf{weight-decay-driven over a flat loss}, not earned.
\textbf{(iii) A language-model control bounds the null.} A 2-layer char-Mamba LM on
prose vs.\ Python, with the domain-supervised regime and the same input-affine
FiLM, does \emph{not} collapse: with a strongly-decodable regime (router
${\sim}0.95$ accuracy) $\mathrm{film}_g$ rises $0.11\!\to\!0.52$. Gauge absorption
always removes the \emph{constant} component; the input-dependent component survives
only when the regime is decodable enough to be worth encoding---so the collapse is
the fate of \emph{weakly-decodable}, drop-in conditioning specifically, the
predictability/volatility axes a financial world model faces. (Single-seed toy
control; the router$\Rightarrow$persistence outcome is clear, the plateau value is
not load-bearing.)

\section{The Substrate Is Not the Subject}\label{sec:substrate}

The financial datasets are a demonstration substrate (genuine non-stationarity is
exactly where conditioning \emph{should} help); the backbone is a Mamba-3 world
model in the DreamerV3/Drama lineage~\citep{hafner2023dreamerv3, wang2024drama}, but
none of that is the claim. Two checks confirm it carries no weight. \textbf{(i) No backbone dominates}: a
matched-parameter ablation (Mamba-1/2/3 SISO and a Mamba-3-matched pre-norm
Transformer) shows no backbone leads on both FI-2010 and Kaggle
(Table~\ref{tab:backbone}, App.~\ref{app:tables})---exactly what a result about how
selective scans \emph{train} predicts. \textbf{(ii) The surviving economic edge is
architecture-independent}: a selective-participation strategy gate turns a
directional settlement probability into a survivable high-predictability edge
($\mathrm{P(profit)}\approx1.0$, 95\% CI $[+\$2{,}445,+\$7{,}747]$ over $127$
markets), but a logistic regression on the same spot-conditioned features
\emph{reproduces and exceeds} it ($+\$6{,}914$ vs.\ the settlement head's
$+\$5{,}147$)---so the operative variable is the features and the gate, not the
sequence model (full numbers in App.~\ref{app:coda}).

\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Scale redundancy and scale-invariance.}
That a channel-wise affine before a linear map is redundant with that map is a known
theme in the normalization literature: \citet{vanlaarhoven2017l2} shows weights
feeding a normalization layer are scale-invariant, so $L_2$ regularization acts on
their \emph{scale} (the effective learning rate) rather than as capacity control,
and \citet{hoffer2018norm} and \citet{li2020exp} re-derive weight decay as an
effective-learning-rate schedule on the same symmetry. Our null is an instance in a
setting it has not been analyzed: the absorbed affine is not a static normalization
gain but a per-regime, \emph{input-dependent} FiLM folding into the selective
scan's own $W_\Delta/W_B/W_C$. What is new is (i) the input-dependent
selective-scan instantiation; (ii) the dataset- and architecture-invariant
signature ($\tau\approx7000$, $R^2>0.999$) attributing the collapse to the
optimizer; (iii) the output-side placement showing the null is
\emph{overdetermined}; and (iv) that the only kernel-friendly, drop-in form is
exactly the absorbable one---the default a practitioner reaches for, not a corner
case. Mamba-2 recasts the scan as structured masked
attention~\citep{dao2024mamba2}; DeepLOB~\citep{zhang2019deeplob} anchors the
in-distribution macro-F1 confirming our data carries signal.

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

The $\le1$-GPU constraint is why this study is conclusive rather than suggestive:
an exact zero-init control is only meaningful if the two runs are otherwise
bit-identical, and forced-active escalations are affordable only because each run is
small. \textbf{Takeaway:} do not gate a selective scan's input (or output) with a
learned affine and expect persistence---it is a gauge the optimizer removes at a
data-independent rate; only placements acting on $W_\Delta/W_B/W_C$ \emph{inside}
the fused kernel survive, the change that breaks drop-in deployment.

\section{Mechanism: the output-side placement in full}\label{app:mech}

The output-side gate applies $y'=\gamma\odot y+\beta$ to the block output. Each
Mamba-3 block ends in a \emph{bias-free} linear output projection
$y=W_{\text{out}}h$, so a channel-wise output scale folds exactly into it,
$\gamma\odot(W_{\text{out}}h)=(\diag(\gamma)W_{\text{out}})h$---the same gauge move
as the input side, on the block's other face. The additive shift $\beta$ is
genuinely non-absorbable (no output bias, a nonlinear $\RMSNorm$ separating it from
the next block's projections), yet under forced activation and ER supervision
$\mathrm{film}_g$ and $\mathrm{film}_b$ decay monotonically and in lockstep on both
datasets ($\mathrm{film}_g: 0.250\!\to\!0.147$ FI-2010, $0.250\!\to\!0.143$ Kaggle;
$\mathrm{film}_b$ tracks within $0.005$), $\mathrm{reg}_H\approx\log 4$ throughout.
The fit gives the same dataset-invariant signature ($a\approx0.30$,
$\tau\approx7065$ FI-2010 / $6598$ Kaggle, asymptote $c\approx-0.05$, $R^2>0.999$).
Two probes localize the failure: the jointly-supervised router is uniform per step
($\approx\log 4$) with near-chance ER agreement ($0.25$--$0.31$), and a frozen
linear probe decodes the predictability bucket only weakly ($0.33$--$0.36$ vs.\
$0.25$) and the volatility bucket somewhat better ($0.43$--$0.47$)---yet the
volatility-axis gap is also null.

\section{Methods detail}\label{app:methods}

All toy experiments use the pure-PyTorch reference scan that \texttt{mamba\_ssm}
ships (the slow path the fused-kernel hardware limit does not touch), in seconds on
the same single 4080. \textbf{Folding}: the recovered projections satisfy
$W_\bullet^{\text{fit}}\approx W_\bullet^{\text{base}}\diag(\gamma)$ to round-off
(input-affine residual $1.2\times10^{-6}$); the in-kernel placement leaves a
residual $1.3\times10^{-2}$ because a per-channel scale on the post-softplus
$\Delta$ cannot be matched by any linear reparameterization of the pre-softplus
projection. \textbf{Dynamics}: input-affine decays $0.086\!\to\!0.010$ (WD on),
in-kernel persists $0.093\!\to\!0.175$ (seeds 0--3). \textbf{Weight-decay
ablation}: WD off leaves the input-affine at its forced-active init (toy
$0.086\!\to\!0.342$; full-model $\tau$ from $\approx6685$, $R^2{=}0.9999$, to
$\approx3\times10^5$, $R^2{=}0.48$). \textbf{LM control}: a 2-layer char-level
Mamba LM (built on \texttt{mamba\_ssm} blocks) on prose vs.\ Python with a
context-aware domain-supervised router and the same input-affine FiLM; router
${\sim}0.95$ accuracy, $\mathrm{film}_g: 0.11\!\to\!0.52$. We run Mamba-3 SISO
throughout because the MIMO kernel's dynamic shared-memory footprint exceeds the
4080 cap at every warp-valid (rank, chunk) configuration---the same kernel region
a surviving modulation would have to occupy.

\section{Deflated economic coda}\label{app:coda}

Given the spot path that defines Polymarket settlement, the model's
over-confident-but-directional settlement probability---traded by a
selective-participation strategy that bets only when a train-frozen
efficiency-ratio gate fires, against a mandatory no-model naive control (which
loses $-\$1{,}008$), summarized by a market-block bootstrap that resamples whole
markets---is a survivable, naive-beating edge in the high-predictability regime
($\mathrm{P(profit)}\approx1.0$, 95\% CI $[+\$2{,}445,+\$7{,}747]$ over $127$
markets). The edge is architecture-independent: a logistic regression on the same
spot-conditioned features reproduces and exceeds it ($+\$6{,}914$, Brier $0.146$,
vs.\ the settlement head's $+\$5{,}147$ and a 3-seed EV-head mean of $+\$4{,}779$;
logistic $\mathrm{P}\approx1.0$, exactly $0.9994$, over $243$ markets). The
settlement head's low-predictability regime is marginal ($+\$51$,
$\mathrm{P}=0.63$) and is made robust by a direct expected-value head
($\mathrm{P}\to0.98$--$0.99$, collapsing the high-vs-low PnL spread $6.2\times$ at
comparable total PnL). Only the predictability gate is confirmatory; the depth-band
and early-market cuts are exploratory, and the reliability/PnL figures are
recorded-vintage structural diagnostics. The deterministic conjunction-gated run
totals $+\$4{,}729$ over $1{,}553$ trades ($408$ markets)---a different denominator
from the $645$-market settlement total and the $243$-market logistic, each labeled
where it appears.
