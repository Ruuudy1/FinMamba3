# Input-Affine Conditioning of Selective State-Space Models Is Gauge-Absorbable: A Diagnostic and a Design Rule for FiLM-Style Regime Conditioning of Mamba

**Anonymous Authors**

## Abstract

Selective state-space models (Mamba) have reshaped long-context sequence modeling, yet which forms of conditioning *survive* their training dynamics is poorly understood — and getting it wrong is a quiet, recurring failure for practitioners. We study FiLM-style regime conditioning of a Mamba backbone — a per-block, channel-wise affine on each block's input, the natural drop-in way to steer the input-dependent selective-scan parameters $\Delta,\mathbf{B},\mathbf{C}$ — and find it a **decisive null** with an **actionable** mechanistic explanation. Under joint training the modulation collapses to identity and cannot be forced active even by direct supervision. The reason is mechanical: an input-side affine is a **gauge** direction that folds losslessly into the block's own $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ projections, so the optimizer slides it back to identity. The signature is dataset- and architecture-invariant: forced-active, efficiency-ratio-supervised escalations decay to identity with a shared time constant ($\tau\approx7000$ steps, $R^2>0.999$) across FI-2010 equities, Kaggle crypto spot books, and Polymarket prediction markets — implicating the optimizer, not the data. An output-side placement sharpens rather than escapes the mechanism: its multiplicative scale folds into the block's *other* (bias-free output) projection and decays with the same constant, while the one non-absorbable component — an additive shift — decays in lockstep, so the null is **overdetermined**. This yields two things a practitioner can act on. (1) A **load-bearing activation diagnostic**: a positive generalization gap reported alongside an inert FiLM ($\mathrm{film}_g=\mathrm{mean}\,|\gamma-1|\!\approx\!0.004$–$0.010$, $\mathrm{reg}_H$ at $\log R$) is an *artifact*, not a conditioning effect — a methodological hazard that must be checked before any "conditioning helped" claim. (2) An **actionable design rule**: input-affine (and output-affine) regime conditioning of a selective scan will not persist; surviving conditioning must modulate $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ *inside* the fused kernel, or it should not be attempted in that form. We demonstrate the result on a financial substrate, with an architecture-agnosticism check (no backbone dominates across datasets), and report a deflated economic coda for completeness.

## 1. Introduction

Conditioning a network on side information — a task ID, a context vector, an inferred regime — is among the most common things a practitioner does, and FiLM (a channel-wise affine $\gamma\odot x+\beta$) [Perez et al. 2018] is the default mechanism. When the backbone is a **selective state-space model** (Mamba [Gu & Dao 2024]), there is an especially tempting place to inject it. Mamba's scan parameters $\Delta_t,\mathbf{B}_t,\mathbf{C}_t$ are *input-dependent* linear projections of each block's input, so an affine on that input flows straight into the dynamics — and, crucially, it needs **no change to the fused CUDA/TileLang kernel**. This is the drop-in form everyone reaches for first. We show it does not work, explain mechanistically why, and turn that explanation into two things a practitioner can use: a diagnostic that catches a specific false-positive, and a design rule that says where a surviving modulation has to live.

**The finding.** We introduce a *RegimeFiLM* modulator that infers a categorical latent regime and emits per-block affine parameters $(\gamma,\beta)$, zero-initialized so the modulated backbone is *exactly* the unmodulated baseline at step 0 (an exact control). Despite the strongest activation pressure we could apply — forced-active initialization plus direct supervision of the router — the modulation decays to identity on every objective and dataset we test. The cause is a **gauge symmetry**: a constant channel-wise scale on the block input folds *exactly* into the host projections $W_\Delta/W_B/W_C$ as a right-multiplied diagonal $W_\bullet\diag(\gamma)$, and the shift folds into their biases, with **no change to the function the block computes**. Identity is therefore a flat, lossless valley the optimizer slides along; joint training returns $(\gamma,\beta)\to(\mathbf{1},\mathbf{0})$. The decay constant is dataset-invariant ($\tau\approx7000$, $R^2>0.999$) precisely because the absorption direction is a property of the *block parameterization*, not the data. Moving the gate to the block *output* does not escape this — the scale folds into the bias-free output projection instead — so the null is overdetermined by gauge absorption (every direction the host weights can represent) plus reconstruction dominance (the one direction they cannot, the non-absorbable shift, which simply earns nothing).

**Why this is actionable.** Two upshots change what a practitioner does.

- *A diagnostic that catches a false-positive.* The activation diagnostics $\mathrm{film}_g=\mathrm{mean}\,|\gamma-1|$ and the router entropy $\mathrm{reg}_H$ (uniform maximum $\log R$) are **load-bearing**. In our data a generalization-gap metric reads *positive* — with the "right" sign — while FiLM is provably inert and the treatment is *absolutely worse* in both regimes; the gap is a distributional ($\mathrm{high}-\mathrm{low}$) artifact whose sign even flips with head calibration. A practitioner who reports "conditioning improved the out-of-regime gap" without checking that the gate is actually *on* can ship an artifact. **Check $\mathrm{film}_g$ and $\mathrm{reg}_H$ before claiming a conditioning effect.**
- *A design rule that says where to put the gate.* If you want regime conditioning of a selective scan to *survive* training, the input-affine and output-affine forms will be folded back to identity. The surviving mechanism must act **between the block's bounding projections** — i.e., modulate $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ inside the fused kernel — or the conditioning should not be attempted in affine form at all. We delineate the boundary not as a parameterization that survives, but as the *location* (inside the kernel) a surviving modulation would have to occupy.

**Demonstration substrate.** To stress the mechanism on genuinely different data we build a Mamba-3 world model for limit-order-book (LOB) microstructure in the DreamerV3/Drama lineage [Hafner et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2024; Lahoti et al. 2026], exercised on FI-2010 equities, Kaggle crypto spot books, and Polymarket prediction markets (§4). The backbone is a substrate, not a claim: under matched parameters *no* backbone dominates across datasets (§5.3) — exactly what a result about how selective scans *train*, rather than which one is best, should predict. The same study yields a deflated economic coda (§6): conditioning the *dynamics* did not help, but a decision rule over the model's outputs is a survivable, naive-beating edge that a logistic regression on the same features reproduces — i.e., not attributable to the architecture. We report it for completeness and to make the substrate concrete; it is not the contribution.

## 2. Related Work

**Selective state-space models and Mamba.** Structured state-space models (S4, S5, Mamba) give $O(n)$-complexity alternatives to Transformers [Gu et al. 2022; Smith et al. 2023; Gu & Dao 2024]. Mamba introduced *selective* state spaces in which $\Delta$ and $\mathbf{B}$ are input-dependent, enabling content-aware filtering; Mamba-2 [Dao & Gu 2024] formalized the duality with structured masked attention, and Mamba-3 [Lahoti et al. 2026] adds multi-input/multi-output (MIMO) transitions. The property we exploit — that $\Delta_t,\mathbf{B}_t,\mathbf{C}_t$ are learned linear projections of the block input — is exactly what makes an input-affine a gauge direction. We are not aware of prior work characterizing the training-time fate of FiLM-style conditioning of a selective scan.

**FiLM and feature-wise conditioning.** FiLM [Perez et al. 2018] injects context as a channel-wise affine and is the default conditioning primitive across vision, multi-task, and regime-embedding settings [Lim et al. 2021]. Our result is about *where* in a selective-scan block an affine can be placed and still survive joint training — a gauge constraint specific to the input-dependent projection structure, orthogonal to FiLM's expressive-power story.

**Interpretability of training dynamics and weight symmetries.** A line of interpretability work studies *how* solutions form rather than *what* a fixed network computes, including reparameterization and gauge symmetries that leave the function unchanged while reshaping the loss landscape. Our diagnosis is in this spirit: identity is a flat valley induced by an exact parameter symmetry (scale-into-projection), and the observed monotone, dataset-invariant decay is the optimizer sliding along it. The actionable contribution is to turn that symmetry into a measurable diagnostic ($\mathrm{film}_g$, $\mathrm{reg}_H$) and a placement rule.

**LOB models and regime-switching (substrate).** DeepLOB [Zhang et al. 2019] and successors are discriminative LOB models on FI-2010 [Ntakaris et al. 2018]; generative LOB work [Nagy et al. 2023; Backhouse et al. 2025] targets simulation fidelity; classical regime-switching identifies discrete states from price processes [Hamilton 1989]. We use these only as the demonstration substrate and benchmark context, not as baselines for the conditioning result.

## 3. Setup: FiLM Conditioning of a Selective Scan

**Selective SSM.** A discretized state-space layer maps $x_t\in\R^{d}$ to $y_t$ via a latent state $h_t$: $h_t=\bar{\mathbf{A}}h_{t-1}+\bar{\mathbf{B}}x_t$, $y_t=\mathbf{C}h_t$. Mamba makes the parameters **input-dependent**:
$$\Delta_t=\sigma(W_\Delta\,x_t),\qquad \mathbf{B}_t=W_B\,x_t,\qquad \mathbf{C}_t=W_C\,x_t,$$
so *any affine transformation of $x_t$ propagates directly into all three scan parameters*. This is the selection mechanism we attempt to steer.

**RegimeFiLM modulator.** A regime head produces a soft categorical $\bm{\alpha}_t=\softmax(W_r\,x_t^{(0)})\in\R^R$ ($R=8$) and a regime embedding $r_t=\bm{\alpha}_t^\top E$; a hypernetwork maps $r_t$ to per-block affine parameters, bounded for bf16 stability:
$$\gamma^{(\ell)}=1+\tanh(\tilde{\gamma}^{(\ell)})\in(0,2),\qquad \beta^{(\ell)}=\tanh(\tilde{\beta}^{(\ell)})\in(-1,1).$$
The hypernetwork is **zero-initialized**, so at step 0 $\gamma^{(\ell)}=\mathbf{1},\beta^{(\ell)}=\mathbf{0}$ for all $\ell$ and the modulated backbone is *exactly* the unmodulated baseline — a clean, exact paired control. A Switch-Transformer load-balancing term [Fedus et al. 2022] maximizes the entropy of the *batch-averaged* regime distribution, $\mathcal{L}_{\text{regime}}=\lambda_{\text{ent}}(\log R-H[\bar{\bm{\alpha}}])$, regularizing marginal usage while leaving per-step assignments free to be peaky.

**Two application sites.** The **input-affine** site routes the regime into the scan through the selection mechanism:
$$x_t^{(\ell)}=x_t^{(\ell-1)}+\mathrm{Mamba3}\!\big(\gamma^{(\ell)}\odot\RMSNorm(x_t^{(\ell-1)})+\beta^{(\ell)}\big).$$
As a control we also expose a **post-scan** site that gates the block *output*:
$$x_t^{(\ell)}=x_t^{(\ell-1)}+\gamma^{(\ell)}\odot\mathrm{Mamba3}\!\big(\RMSNorm(x_t^{(\ell-1)})\big)+\beta^{(\ell)}.$$
Both share the identical modulator; only the site differs. This isolates the gauge structure (§5): the input-side scale folds into $W_\Delta/W_B/W_C$; the post-scan scale folds into the bias-free output projection $W_{\text{out}}$; only the post-scan additive shift is non-absorbable.

**Diagnostics.** Two activation metrics gate every claim: $\mathrm{film}_g=\mathrm{mean}\,|\gamma-1|$ (deviation of the multiplicative gate from identity) and the router entropy $\mathrm{reg}_H$ (uniform maximum $\log R$; $\log 4$ on FI-2010, $\log 8$ on Polymarket). **A positive generalization gap with an inert FiLM is an artifact, not a regime-conditioning effect** — the central methodological point.

## 4. Demonstration Substrate (condensed)

To exercise the mechanism on genuinely different distributions we instantiate the modulated backbone inside a Mamba-3 world model for LOB microstructure (DreamerV3/Drama lineage [Hafner et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2024]). Briefly: a Transformer-over-depth encoder maps each LOB snapshot (10 depth levels as tokens, plus tick-level aggregates and — on Polymarket — binary-market invariants: boundary distance, logit-space velocity, Amihud illiquidity, variance ratio) to an embedding; a $16\times16$ categorical posterior feeds a stem and an $L{=}4$ stack of Mamba-3 SISO blocks ($d_{\text{state}}{=}128$, RoPE on 50% of the head dim); a decoder reconstructs the features under a weighted-MSE or Student-$t$ likelihood. The model is trained with the standard DreamerV3 world-model loss (reconstruction + dynamics/representation KL with a free-bits floor) plus light auxiliary heads. *Auxiliary machinery not central to this paper — episodic memory, a Hawkes intensity head, the five Lopez-de-Prado bar types, the POMDP/Phase-B imagination RL loop, and the EV-head internals — is described in the released code and demoted here to a sentence each; none of the conditioning results depends on any of it.*

**Regime axis.** Beyond realized volatility we use a **predictability** axis on the underlying spot series — Kaufman efficiency ratio $\mathrm{ER}=|s_n-s_0|/\sum_t|s_t-s_{t-1}|$ (near 1 a clean trend, near 0 a random wander), permutation entropy, and Hurst exponent — both to split the held-out evaluation into forecastable vs. random-walk regimes and, when the router collapses, to *supervise it directly*. "Is this window forecastable" is the most defensible regime signal in the project; a router that will not encode even this axis is the strongest form of the null.

**Datasets.** (i) Polymarket binary-outcome crypto LOBs (BTC/ETH/SOL up/down at 5/15/60-min intervals, with the underlying Chainlink/Binance spot path); (ii) the public FI-2010 benchmark [Ntakaris et al. 2018]; (iii) Kaggle high-frequency crypto spot books (BTC/ETH/ADA at 1 s / 1 min / 5 min). Held-out markets are split into reference and shifted regimes by predictability (primary) and realized volatility (secondary); both reported, not pooled. **All thresholds are fit on the training split and frozen before any held-out evaluation.** Baseline and treatment share the seed, so the zero-init FiLM is an exact paired control.

### 5.2 The mechanism: gauge absorption, and why it is overdetermined

**Cross-dataset invariance points at the optimizer.** We take the architecture finding to FI-2010 (re-examined under the predictability-primary axis) and the Kaggle crypto spot book (BTC/ETH/ADA at three resolutions), judged by forecasting metrics alone. The zero-initialized modulation never leaves identity: $\mathrm{film}_g\approx0.004$ and $\mathrm{reg}_H=\log 4$ in every setting (Table 2). With FiLM an exact paired control, every held-out gap is statistically indistinguishable from zero (all 95% CIs include 0) on both regime axes. The split is non-degenerate: high-efficiency-ratio reference windows carry genuinely more structure on all three predictability measures, and DeepLOB attains $0.628$ macro-F1 on the FI-2010 horizon-10 labels — so the null is a negative on *forecastable* data, not a no-signal artifact.

**Why identity is a free direction: gauge absorption.** Our modulation is an input-side affine, $\tilde{x}=\gamma\odot\hat{x}+\beta$, applied to a block input that the very next operations re-project: $\Delta_t=\sigma(W_\Delta\tilde{x}_t)$, $\mathbf{B}_t=W_B\tilde{x}_t$, $\mathbf{C}_t=W_C\tilde{x}_t$. A constant channel-wise scale folds *exactly* into those projections as a right-multiplied diagonal, $W_\bullet\diag(\gamma)$, and the shift folds into their biases, with **no change to the function the block computes**; the host weights can thus represent any constant $(\gamma,\beta)$ themselves. Identity is therefore a **gauge** direction — a flat, lossless valley the optimizer slides along — so joint training folds the modulation into the host weights and returns $(\gamma,\beta)\to(\mathbf{1},\mathbf{0})$, which is precisely the monotone decay we observe. This explains the dataset-invariant decay constant (the absorption direction is a property of the block parameterization, not the data) and *scopes* the claim: **input-affine conditioning of a selective scan is gauge-absorbable and will not persist under joint training** — not that regime conditioning is impossible in principle. The actionable negative is that the most kernel-friendly, drop-in form of the idea is exactly the form the optimizer can and does undo.

**An output-side placement: the null is overdetermined.** The gauge argument names an obvious next test — gating *after* the scan — so we run it under the identical forced-active, ER-supervised escalation, changing only where $(\gamma,\beta)$ are applied. This does *not* escape absorption, and the reason is instructive. Each Mamba-3 block ends in a **bias-free** output projection $y=W_{\text{out}}h$, so a channel-wise output scale folds exactly into it, $\gamma\odot(W_{\text{out}}h)=(\diag(\gamma)W_{\text{out}})h$ — the same gauge move on the block's *other* face. The additive shift $\beta$ is different: with no output bias to absorb it and a nonlinear RMSNorm separating it from the next block's projections, $\beta$ is genuinely **non-absorbable**. The escalation resolves both at once: under forced activation and direct ER supervision, $\mathrm{film}_g$ *and* $\mathrm{film}_b$ decay monotonically and in lockstep on both datasets ($\mathrm{film}_g$: $0.250\to0.147$ FI-2010, $0.250\to0.143$ Kaggle; $\mathrm{film}_b$ tracks within $0.005$), $\mathrm{reg}_H\approx\log 4$ throughout. The exponential fit gives the *same* dataset-invariant signature ($a\approx0.30$, $\tau\approx7065$ FI-2010 / $6598$ Kaggle, asymptote $c\approx-0.05$, $R^2>0.999$). The null is therefore **overdetermined** by two independent mechanisms: **gauge absorption** disposes of every direction the host weights can represent — the multiplicative scale on *either* face of the block, which is why the decay constant is placement-invariant — and **reconstruction dominance** disposes of the one direction they cannot, the non-absorbable shift $\beta$, returned to identity at the same rate purely because it earns nothing under the objective. A modulation escaping both would have to act *between* the block's bounding projections — modulating $W_\Delta/W_B/W_C$ inside the fused selective-scan kernel.

**The boundary, demonstrated — and a gauge-vs-regularization control.** Two cheap experiments turn the design rule from asserted to demonstrated. *(i) Folding.* On a toy pure-PyTorch reference scan, an input-affine modulation folds into the host projections to floating-point precision (max output deviation $1.2\times10^{-6}$) and decays to identity under joint training ($\mathrm{film}_g: 0.086\to0.010$), while the *same* modulation applied to the projected $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ *inside* the scan is provably non-absorbable (best-fit fold residual $1.3\times10^{-2}$, $\sim10^4\times$ larger) and **persists** ($\mathrm{film}_g: 0.093\to0.175$, seeds 0–3). So the in-kernel target the design rule names is real: a modulation placed there *survives* the training that decays the affine form. *(ii) Weight-decay control.* Rerunning the full-model escalation with weight decay removed flattens the decay — the time constant blows up from $\tau\approx6685$ ($R^2=0.9999$) to $\tau\approx3\times10^5$ ($R^2=0.48$, no clean decay) — so the gauge-direction decay is driven by **weight decay over a flat loss**, not by the objective. Actionably: the collapse is not a hyperparameter you can tune away by dropping weight decay (the loss is flat along the gauge direction either way), and it is absent for the non-absorbable in-kernel placement.

**Decodable yet unused — and not even needed.** Two probes localize the failure. A post-hoc reading of the jointly-supervised router shows it is uniform *per step* (per-sample entropy $\approx\log 4$) with near-chance agreement against the efficiency-ratio bucket ($0.25$–$0.31$ vs. $0.25$): direct supervision does not even make the router regime-discriminative. A frozen linear probe on the unmodulated features decodes the per-step predictability bucket only weakly ($0.33$–$0.36$ vs. $0.25$ chance) and the volatility bucket somewhat better ($0.43$–$0.47$) — yet the volatility-axis gap is *also* null. So even on the axis the representation encodes best, and even when the regime is supplied by supervision, the in-scan modulation collapses to identity. (The degenerate next-tick direction head is de-confounded by inverse-frequency class balancing, which yields a non-degenerate macro-F1 with the gap still null; the single borderline gap, Kaggle spot-vol at $n{=}2$, is disproved by a pre-registered third seed that flips its sign.)

**When conditioning *does* survive: regime decodability.** The collapse is not universal, and the boundary is itself actionable. A Mamba char-LM control on a two-domain corpus (English prose vs. Python source), with the regime inferred by a context-aware router supervised on the domain and the *same* input-affine FiLM, does **not** collapse: with a strongly-decodable regime (router $\sim0.95$ domain accuracy) $\mathrm{film}_g$ *rises* from $0.11$ to $0.52$ and plateaus. Gauge absorption always removes the *constant* part of the affine; the *input-dependent* part survives only when the regime is decodable enough to be worth encoding. In the LOB settings the predictability/volatility regime is weakly decodable and the router collapses to uniform, so the modulation reduces to its gauge-absorbable constant part and decays. **The actionable rule, refined:** the input-affine FiLM is safe to skip exactly when the regime is weakly decodable from the block input; if your regime is strongly and contextually decodable, the affine *can* engage — so the two-number diagnostic ($\mathrm{film}_g$, $\mathrm{reg}_H$) also tells you whether your conditioning sits in the regime where it will be absorbed.

**The gap is real, and FiLM closes none of it.** The null is not an absence of anything to fix. On the Kaggle spot book, full-channel reconstruction NLL is $+0.105$ worse on the high-volatility (shifted) windows than on the low-volatility (reference) windows, in *both* arms. RegimeFiLM is designed precisely to absorb such shifts, yet it narrows the degradation by $\sim0.001$ (a $\sim1\%$ sliver) while $\mathrm{film}_g$ is provably inert. The architecture is not failing for want of a gap to close; it is failing to engage a gap that is plainly present.

### 5.3 Architecture-agnosticism check (matched-parameter backbone ablation)

We compare Mamba-1, Mamba-2, Mamba-3 (SISO), and a Transformer-over-time under matched parameter budgets ($\sim10.3$M) on FI-2010 and the Kaggle BTC spot book, reporting held-out full-channel Student-$t$ reconstruction NLL (lower is better). The result is a genuine ambiguity: **no backbone leads on both datasets** — Mamba-1 best on FI-2010, Mamba-2 (fewest SSM params) best on Kaggle, Mamba-3 SISO competitive but not strictly dominant. A pre-norm Transformer matched to the Mamba-3 block (RMSNorm, SiLU, $4\times$ FFN; $9.61$M) is likewise competitive but non-dominant ($-0.5041$ FI-2010, $0.0224$ Kaggle), so the non-dominance is not an artifact of an under-tuned post-norm baseline. This is exactly what a result about how selective scans *train*, rather than which one is best, should predict.

This is the same kernel boundary the design rule names — the surviving, in-kernel modulation of $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ lives behind the same fused-kernel wall.

## 6. Deflated Economic Coda (substrate, one section)

The substrate yields a positive result the architecture did *not* carry, which we report honestly as a deflated coda. Given the underlying spot path that **defines** Polymarket settlement, the world model's directionally-informative settlement probability — traded by a **selective-participation** strategy that bets only when a causal predictability gate fires (rolling ER above a train-frozen threshold) and is benchmarked against a mandatory no-model `NaiveLag` control — is a survivable, naive-beating edge on held-out BTC: $+\$4{,}729$ (deterministic-latent, 1,553 trades), $6$–$9\times$ a fair trend-following naive, P(profit)$=1.0$. A dependence-respecting **market-block bootstrap** (resampling whole markets, since trades inside one market share a settlement outcome) places the high-predictability edge away from zero: P(profit)$=1.0$, 95% CI $[+\$2{,}445,\,+\$7{,}747]$ over $127$ independent traded markets.

**Crucially, the edge is architecture-independent.** A logistic regression on the *same* spot-conditioned features, traded through the *identical* gate/sizing/engine/bootstrap, reproduces and *exceeds* it: $+\$6{,}914$ at Brier $0.146$ (vs. the settlement head's $+\$5{,}147$ / Brier $0.206$), survivable under the market-block bootstrap (P(profit)$=0.999$ over $243$ independent markets, 95% CI lower bound $+\$2{,}844$). Binary-contract settlement is a near-deterministic function of the spot move since open, and a linear readout captures it at least as cleanly as the world model's head — so the operative variable is the spot-conditioned pipeline and the gate, **not the sequence model**. (Stated both ways: the world model is a generative dynamics model a logistic cannot replace; the baseline shows only that *this readout-and-trade* does not need it.)

**Regime robustness via a decision rule.** Broader coverage (645 markets) exposes a regime *weakness* in the settlement head: low-predictability markets earn only $+\$51$ at P(profit)$=0.63$. Supervising the *tradeable* expected value directly — a `BookRelativeEdgeHead` trained with Huber loss on $e_{\text{yes}}=\text{outcome}-\text{ask}_{\text{yes}}$, $e_{\text{no}}=(1-\text{outcome})-\text{ask}_{\text{no}}$, so the target is near zero (abstain) when the book is already fair — makes the low-predictability regime robust: low-pred $+\$2{,}368$ at P(profit)$=0.99$ (seed 0); three-seed mean $+\$4{,}779$ total at mean low-pred P(profit)$=0.98$, collapsing the high-vs-low PnL spread $6.2\times$ ($\$5{,}044\to\$814$) at total PnL comparable to settlement ($+\$5{,}147$). The same lesson the null teaches, from the other side: conditioning the *dynamics* did not help, but a decision rule over the model's outputs did. (We note the honest boundary: the same-feature logistic is *already* regime-robust — high-pred $+\$2{,}448$, low-pred $+\$4{,}466$, both P(profit)$\ge0.99$ — so the low-predictability signal is not intrinsically hard; the EV head is a real fix *for the world-model readout*, not the only way to capture it.)

*Figure 3 (`figures/reliability_settlement.pdf`). **Settlement-head reliability** over $214$k held-out probabilities (Brier $0.206$). Points fall below the diagonal for mean $>0.5$ — systematic over-confidence — yet the populous extreme deciles land on the correct side of $0.5$ ($0.787$ realized in the top decile, $0.109$ in the bottom), the directional signal the divergence trade harvests; mid-confidence bins ($0.5$–$0.9$) realize below $0.5$, the calibration footprint of the low-predictability failure the EV head removes.*

*Figure 4 (`figures/simple_baseline_pnl.pdf`). **The economic edge does not need the architecture.** Held-out PnL of the same-feature simple models on the $645$-market set, with market-block 95% confidence intervals. The logistic's $+\$6{,}914$ exceeds both world-model heads, and its market-block interval ($+\$2{,}844$ to $+\$11$k over $243$ independent markets) survives the dependence-respecting bootstrap.*

**Honest scope.** The economic edge is BTC-centric and a latency effect (it harvests the lag between the underlying spot and the contract book), stress-tested by delaying the model's signal and by walk-the-book fills that assume recorded liquidity is takeable, so live market impact would erode some of it. **Evidential status is stated explicitly:** only the train-frozen predictability gate is *confirmatory*; the depth-band and early-market cuts are *exploratory* held-out characterizations subject to forking-paths caveats. The settlement-head reliability table (Figure 3) and the underlying PnL totals are **recorded-vintage structural diagnostics** — the over-confidence pattern is structural, not vintage-specific. The coda exists to make the substrate concrete and to show the architecture is *not* the operative variable; it is not the contribution.

## 7. Discussion: What a Practitioner Should Do Differently

The interpretability finding is small and exact — an input-affine on a Mamba block input is a gauge direction — but it has two consequences a practitioner can act on immediately.

**Actionable takeaway.** *If you want regime (or any side-information) conditioning of a Mamba selective scan to survive training, do not use an input-affine or output-affine FiLM: both are gauge directions the optimizer provably folds back into the host $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ (or bias-free output) projections and decays to identity ($\tau\approx7000$, $R^2>0.999$, invariant across datasets and placement) — modulate $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ inside the fused kernel, or do not bother; and before reporting that any conditioning "helped," verify the gate is actually on ($\mathrm{film}_g=\mathrm{mean}\,|\gamma-1|$ clearly $>0$, router entropy $\mathrm{reg}_H$ off its uniform maximum $\log R$), because a positive generalization gap measured against an inert gate is a distributional artifact whose sign can flip with calibration.*

**The diagnostic generalizes.** The two-number check ($\mathrm{film}_g$, $\mathrm{reg}_H$) is cheap, architecture-agnostic, and catches a specific, seductive false-positive: an ablation that "turns conditioning on" and reports a better out-of-regime gap, where the gap moved for distributional reasons and the conditioner never engaged. Any conditioning study on a selective scan should log it.

**The design rule names a target, not a dead end.** Gauge absorption removes every affine direction the host weights can represent; reconstruction dominance removes the residual non-absorbable shift. What remains — modulating the scan parameters *between* the block's bounding projections, inside the fused kernel — is the location a surviving modulation must occupy, and §5.2 confirms at toy scale that a modulation placed there *does* survive the training that decays the affine form. That is a kernel-engineering task (it needs the shared-memory headroom the SISO/MIMO boundary in §5.3 also runs into), and it is the natural next experiment.

**Limitations.** The decay is established at $\tau\approx7000$ over our training horizon; the exponential fit's asymptote $c\le0$ extrapolates *through* identity, but we do not claim a positive near-identity plateau is impossible under every optimizer/schedule. The economic coda is a backtest substrate with the caveats above; it is not part of the interpretability claim.

## 8. Conclusion

The drop-in, kernel-friendly way to push a regime signal into a Mamba selective scan — an input-affine FiLM on the block input — is a **gauge direction** the optimizer provably folds back into the host projections, so it silently decays to identity, with a dataset- and placement-invariant signature ($\tau\approx7000$, $R^2>0.999$) across three datasets and across input-side and output-side placement; the null is overdetermined by gauge absorption plus reconstruction dominance. The actionable upshot is a **load-bearing activation diagnostic** (a positive generalization gap with an inert FiLM is an artifact to be checked) and an **actionable design rule** (modulate $\Delta/\mathbf{B}/\mathbf{C}$ inside the fused kernel, or do not attempt affine regime conditioning of a selective scan). A financial substrate demonstrates the mechanism, an architecture-agnosticism check confirms no backbone is the operative variable, and a deflated economic coda — reproduced by a logistic regression on the same features — shows the same lesson from the decision-rule side: conditioning the dynamics did not help; a rule over the outputs did.
